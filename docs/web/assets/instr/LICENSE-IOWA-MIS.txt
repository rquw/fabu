Trumpet, Flute and Saxophone samples
====================================

Source: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios,
        Musical Instrument Samples (MIS)
        https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
Recorded by Lawrence Fritts, Director of the Electronic Music Studios and
Professor of Composition, University of Iowa.

Terms, quoted from the MIS website:

  "Since 1997, these recordings have been freely available on this website and
   may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

What was done to them: the originals are chromatic scales recorded note by note
at several dynamic levels. The mezzo-forte, non-vibrato takes were split on
silence into single notes, a handful were kept to cover each instrument's range,
and those were trimmed to about 2.6 seconds, faded and encoded to MP3.

The pitch of every note was measured rather than assumed, and the measured
values are what fabu uses as sample roots, so each instrument plays at its
recorded tuning.
