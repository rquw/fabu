where the samples came from
===========================

Trumpet, Flute, Saxophone
  University of Iowa MIS. Free for any use, see LICENSE-IOWA-MIS.txt.

Pianos, Vibraphone, Glockenspiel, Harp, Organ
  VCSL, CC0. github.com/sgossner/VCSL

Guitars and Bass
  FreePats, CC0. freepats.zenvoid.org
  VCSL has no guitars or bass, that is why these come from elsewhere.

Electric   github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-clean, bridge, small
           clean on purpose, the dirt comes from the Amp effect

traps
-----
Roots are MEASURED off the files. Do not trust the filenames. The Yamaha and
Pipe Organ sets number their octaves differently from the Steinway set, and the
file Yamaha calls C0 is actually A#0, which is why upright_as0 has root 22.18.

Bass only covers E1 to D#2. Above that it gets stretched.

making them
-----------
  ffmpeg -i src.wav -ac 1 -ar 32000 mono.wav
  ffmpeg -i mono.wav -t 9 -af "afade=t=out:st=8.5:d=0.5" -codec:a libmp3lame -b:a 96k out.mp3

Do the downmix as its own step. In one command ffmpeg normalises differently
and everything comes out louder. Organ is 44100 and 2.6s, piano_c1 is 18s
because a low C rings for ages.
