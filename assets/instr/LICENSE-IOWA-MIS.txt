Trumpet, Flute and Saxophone samples
====================================

Source: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios,
        Musical Instrument Samples (MIS)
        https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
Recorded by Lawrence Fritts, Director of the Electronic Music Studios and
Professor of Composition, University of Iowa.

Terms, quoted from the MIS website:

  "Since 1997, these recordings have been freely available on this website and
   may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

Trimmed to about 2.6 seconds, faded, encoded to mp3. Pitches were measured
rather than assumed, and the measured values are what fabu uses as roots.
